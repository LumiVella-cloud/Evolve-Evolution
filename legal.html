Privacy notice

Evolve-Evolution operates a wellness platform from South Africa. The operator determines how personal information is used. Contact us through the website contact channel. The responsible party is Gabrielle Shana Simon, trading as Evolve-Evolution, 46 Kinfauns Street, Sydenham, Johannesburg, South Africa. Information Officer registration and dedicated privacy email are pending.

Information we process

Account name, email, optional telephone number, authentication and security records, booking and manual payment status, contact preferences, access-code activity, assigned programmes and library progress. Optional wellness entries may include measurements, exercise, meals, sleep, mood, stress and personal reflections. These may reveal sensitive health information. Do not enter medical records or information about other people.

Why we process it

We use account and booking information to provide requested services, secure accounts, manage bookings and respond to enquiries. Optional marketing requires a separate choice. We intend to process sensitive wellness information only with an appropriate lawful basis and applicable special-information authorisation, including explicit consent where required. Registration acceptance alone is not consent to all processing.

Access and service providers

Supabase provides authentication, database and media storage; Brevo delivers account emails and may process delivery and engagement information; GitHub Pages hosts the website; esm.sh supplies browser software; Unsplash supplies imagery; WhatsApp handles messages you choose to send. These providers may process technical or account information as relevant to their role. Authorised staff access information needed for their duties; trainers receive assigned programme information, not general private wellness journals through the intended portal. Access is subject to security controls, which cannot guarantee absolute security.

International transfers and retention

Providers may process information outside South Africa. Applicable transfer safeguards and provider locations must be verified before launch. Information should be retained only as necessary for the service, security, legal obligations and disputes. Proposed retention targets, subject to legal and technical verification: active account and wellness records while needed for membership; unverified registrations 30 days; inactive accounts reviewed after 12 months; closed account deletion targeted within 30 days subject to lawful exceptions; financial and tax records five years or longer where required; booking, marketing consent and privacy request records three years after the relevant event where necessary; security logs 90 days; rolling backups 30 days where supported. These targets are not yet automated.

Marketing

Registration does not opt you into advertising. Email and WhatsApp preferences are separate and can be changed in the Client Portal. Withdrawal does not affect necessary account or booking communications.

Membership terms

General membership and available wellness tools are free. Verified accounts are required for member services. Keep credentials private, use accurate account details and do not misuse the platform, attempt unauthorised access or redistribute restricted content. Staff accounts require additional authentication.

Access codes are subject to their stated validity and conditions; they do not automatically create a paid subscription or guarantee a discount on a particular booking. Content and services may change with reasonable notice where appropriate. Nothing here excludes mandatory consumer rights or liability that cannot lawfully be excluded.

Membership is restricted to adults aged 18 and over. South African law applies subject to mandatory protections in the consumer's jurisdiction.

Fitness, nutrition & wellness disclaimer

General educational information, workout ideas and nutrition guidance are not medical diagnosis, treatment or individual clinical dietary advice. Consult an appropriately qualified healthcare professional about injuries, pregnancy, medical conditions, medication, eating disorders or significant dietary changes. Stop exercise if you experience concerning symptoms and seek appropriate care. In an emergency contact local emergency services. Results vary; no particular health, weight or fitness outcome is guaranteed. Individual services must remain within the provider's verified qualifications and lawful scope of practice.

Bookings, cancellations & refunds

Registered members can view applicable prices before booking. Private requests are subject to confirmation. Payments are arranged manually; the platform records payment status and does not collect card details. A recorded refund status is not proof that funds have been transferred.

Use the available group or private session cancellation controls where eligible, or contact us for payment reconciliation and refund requests. Proposed launch terms: cancellations more than 24 hours before a session qualify for a full refund or free rescheduling. Within 24 hours a reasonable cancellation charge of up to 50% may apply; for no-shows a reasonable charge up to the booking amount may apply. Charges are assessed individually and are not automatic. No cancellation charge applies where the intended participant dies or is hospitalised. If we cancel, choose a full refund or free rescheduling. Duplicate or excess payments are refundable. Approved refunds are targeted for initiation within seven business days. Free membership cancellation carries no fee. Applicable cooling-off rights override these terms. Applicable statutory cancellation and refund rights remain unaffected. Confirm specific service arrangements before payment.

Cookies & browser storage

Authentication uses browser storage to maintain sessions. Essential storage supports account security and functionality. External providers may process network information when resources load or links are opened. No optional website analytics or advertising tracking is introduced by this upgrade. Transactional email delivery and engagement tracking may be performed by Brevo; the final tracking configuration is pending verification. Clearing browser storage may sign you out. A full cookie and storage inventory is pending verification.

Your personal information rights

Subject to applicable law, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction or objection, withdraw consent and complain to the relevant regulator. Contact us via the website contact channel and request a secure method to submit a privacy request. Do not send passwords, identity documents, health records or verification codes through the general contact form. We will need to verify identity before releasing or deleting information. Some records may need to be retained by law. Verified members may open privacy and account deletion requests in the Client Portal. Requests are reviewed manually; closing a request does not itself delete data. Separate optional wellness consent and withdrawal are recorded in the portal. During this staged rollout, wellness storage restrictions are not yet enforced; contact us to arrange cessation of processing or deletion.

South African privacy complaints may be directed to the Information Regulator. International rights depend on applicable law, including GDPR where its territorial scope is met.
