# V20B privacy operations — draft, not launch approval

Keep actual requests, identities, incident evidence, credentials and backups outside this public repository.

## Before launch
- Confirm responsible party legal name, service address, Information Officer registration and secure contact route. Review PAIA manual obligations.
- Inventory mandatory and optional data, purposes, lawful bases, special information authorisation, retention and deletion. Conduct a personal information impact assessment.
- Approve age eligibility and professional scope; review CPA/ECTA service terms and refunds.
- Confirm vendor contracts, regions, subprocessors, transfer safeguards and transactional email tracking. Assess applicable international laws.
- Approve versioned policy text, preserve exact historical versions, and change staged SQL version before production if appropriate. Integrate explicit verified-user acknowledgement; do not infer from mutable signup metadata. Separate optional health consent and withdrawal from membership and marketing. Enforce applicable health permissions on the database before collecting new sensitive entries.
## Rights requests
Receive through approved contact route, verify identity proportionately through a secure channel, record request privately, identify applicable deadlines and exemptions, locate data across providers and backups, securely deliver access/correction or perform authorised deletion, record completion. Do not promise unconditional immediate deletion. Preserve legally required records and manage backup expiry. No automatic destructive account deletion.
## Incident response
Contain access, preserve evidence securely, assess affected categories and people, revoke compromised sessions or credentials, contact providers and Information Officer, assess POPIA section 22 notification obligations promptly, notify regulator and affected people as required, document remediation and review.
## Tests
Use synthetic accounts in staging: populated owner/other client rows for every wellness table, booking, contact, code and payment record; check read/write denial, trainer AAL1/AAL2 and assignment boundaries, admin AAL1/AAL2, trigger attachment and booking constraints. Verify private media publication gates, consent history and withdrawal, explicit ledger acknowledgement with server timestamp and unsupported version rejection, anonymous denial and no direct mutation. Verify real browser sessions and restoration from backups in isolated environment. Regress free membership, all five codes, group/private prices, cancellation and manual payments. No gateway or subscriptions.
